What happens when a patient writes to your clinic

Step 1

The patient asks

Opening hours, services, fees, insurance, what to do about a lost filling. The bot replies right away using answers you approved.

Step 2

The bot points to a booking

It shares your booking link, or asks for a name and phone number if the patient prefers a call back.

Step 3

Your team gets an email

The patient's name, number and question land in your inbox, so the front desk can follow up the next morning.

What the bot doesGeneral questions, hours, services, location, booking links, collecting contact details for a call back.
What the bot never doesDiagnose, give treatment advice or quote a clinical outcome. Those questions go to your dentists, and urgent symptoms are pointed to emergency care.
